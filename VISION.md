# DealiX Vision

DealiX is a premium but practical operating system for PC flipping. The app combines build tracking, inventory awareness, testing, marketplace planning, and AI guidance in one dark modern workspace.

## Current product direction

- Keep the experience useful for real workflow, not just mock screens.
- Use local browser storage for editable demo data so the app feels interactive right away.
- Reuse shared UI patterns and keep the design consistent.
- Prepare for future marketplace and AI integrations without connecting them yet.

## Planned maturity layers

- Build management and detail pages
- Parts and inventory tracking
- Testing and benchmarking workflow
- Sales and profit tracking
- Smart compatibility assistance for blocked builds
- Future marketplace and AI features
