# AI Rules

DealiX is preparing for AI assistance, but the current product uses mock responses and placeholder workflows.

## Current AI behavior

- The AI Assistant page offers a chat-style mock interface.
- Responses are demo-only and do not call a paid API yet.
- The app is prepared for future AI features such as deal scoring, listing generation, and negotiation help.

## Future AI direction

- Add explanation-based recommendations for compatibility and pricing.
- Use local mock logic first before connecting any real AI provider.
- Keep the app beginner-friendly and transparent about what is estimated.
