# Business Notes

DealiX is being shaped as a practical operating system for PC flipping. The app focuses on helping a user move from sourcing parts to build completion, testing, listing, and sales tracking.

## Current business goals

- Keep the workflow simple and visual.
- Use local mock data first so the app can be tested quickly.
- Preserve the current dark premium look.
- Prepare the app for real marketplaces and AI features later.
